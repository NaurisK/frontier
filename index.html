Connective visual atlas · M7004R Re:Form

How gaming communities make skill teachable.

Competitive play is situated and dynamic. Communities compress it into diagrams, tables, maps and interfaces that circulate, mutate and are disputed across platforms. Frontier connects selected lineages, contributors and reduction trade-offs without claiming to replace the communities that produced them.

Research question

How might a connective visual atlas make the provenance, adaptation and omissions of competitive-game skill frameworks visible while preserving the simplification that makes them useful?

Who it is for

Primary audience: community guide creators, wiki contributors, coaches and analysts who produce or maintain gaming knowledge resources.

Secondary audience: players and researchers interested in how competitive skill is represented. The principal route is also written to remain understandable to a viewer without specialist gaming knowledge.
